# authix
A Free Extension That Lets You Stay Secured. How to install: 1. Click on releases, and click on the latest release. Then press the .zip file to download it. 2. Extract the .zip file. 3. Go into your browser extension menu and turn on "Developer Mode". Then press "load unpacked" and that's it! OUR WEBSITE: https://sites.google.com/view/authixweb
